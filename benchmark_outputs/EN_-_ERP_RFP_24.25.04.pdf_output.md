### contract_term
The term of the licensing and support agreement is to be for a period of five years, with an option in favour of Cowichan Tribes to extend the agreement on the same terms and conditions.

---
### scope_of_deliverables
Procure a cloud-based ERP system(s) to enhance operational efficiency across key functions including finance, procurement, human resources, payroll, and asset management. The solution should be aligned with industry best practice, provide features to automate processes, provide staff and leadership access to the information needed to effectively plan and operate, and be adaptable to evolving business needs. The scope includes data conversion costs for Finance (detailed transactions for one year, summarized balances for previous 3 years), Procurement (Vendors Records: 10,000), HR (Active Employee Records: 500), Payroll (Current year-to-date payroll data), and Asset Management (Asset Records: 250).

### mandatory_technical_requirements
1. Support for organizations operating in Canada including support for Canadian taxes and Canadian Payroll. 2. Vendor has experience implementing solutions for First Nations in Canada. 3. Solution must be hosted in Canada and stores all data within Canada. 4. Implementation vendor(s) can demonstrate they have at least five years' experience implementing Finance, HR, Payroll, and Asset Management solutions. 5. Solution is cloud based. 6. The solutions are SOC2 Type 2 compliant or equivalent.

### evaluation_criteria
Functional Fit (Requirements Worksheet): 35 points, Threshold 28 (80%); Narrative Proposal: 20 points, Threshold 14 (70%); Pricing: 15 points, Threshold 10.5 (70%); Vendor Demonstration: 30 points, Threshold 21 (70%); Total Points: 100.

### minimum_score_threshold
28 (80%) for Functional Fit; 14 (70%) for Narrative Proposal; 10.5 (70%) for Pricing; 21 (70%) for Vendor Demonstration.

---
### pricing_requirements
Pricing is worth 15 points of the total score. Rates must be provided in Canadian funds, inclusive of all applicable duties and taxes. Rates quoted must be all-inclusive and must include all labour and material costs, all travel and carriage costs, all insurance costs, all costs of delivery, all costs of installation and set-up, including any pre-delivery inspection charges, and all other overhead, including any fees or other charges required by law. Proponents should submit pricing information by completing the attached Pricing Sheet and including it in their proposals. This includes details for implementing and supporting the solution over a 5-year period.

Provide a list of third-party vendors who support cybersecurity practices within the organization. The solutions are SOC2 Type 2 compliant or equivalent.

### vendor_demonstration_requirement
Vendors selected for the demonstration stage will be required to present their proposed solutions in accordance with the demo script that will be provided by Cowichan Tribes. The script will outline the key functionalities and scenarios that must be addressed during the demonstration. To successfully pass this stage, vendors must demonstrate how their solution meets the specified requirements and effectively addresses the use cases outlined in the script. Failure to follow the script or demonstrate the required capabilities may impact the evaluation and progression to the next stage. Vendor Demonstration is worth 30 points with a threshold of 21 (70%).
