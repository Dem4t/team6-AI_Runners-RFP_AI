### contract_term
five (5) years, with an option in favour of the City to extend the agreement on the same terms and conditions annually for an additional two (2) years.

### scope_of_deliverables
A modern, user-friendly, and secure Corporate Learning Management System (LMS) to support corporate training, onboarding, compliance, safety, professional development, and certification tracking for employees, volunteers, and other external learners. The LMS must be available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and support learning and development for approximately 1,500 employees across various departments. The proposed solution must be scalable to accommodate future growth, including increases in user licensing and evolving training and development requirements.

### evaluation_criteria
Product Demonstration: Stage 1 initial evaluation includes a technical component worth 50 points, with a minimum technical score of 35 out of 50 points to meet the technical threshold. Stage 2 demonstrations will be scored out of 30 points and added to the Stage 1 scores to determine the final ranking (Total = 130 points). Rated Criteria Category: Technical Capability (50 points, minimum threshold of 35), Vendor Experience & References (15 points), Implementation & Support (20 points), Pricing (15 points).

### minimum_score_threshold
35 out of 50 points for the technical threshold.

### pricing_requirements
All prices must be in Canadian Dollars (CAD), exclusive of GST. Proposals must clearly separate one-time implementation costs from annual recurring costs.

### minimum_insurance_requirements
Commercial General Liability Insurance with policy limits of not less than Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000.00) per occurrence with a property damage deductible not exceeding Two Thousand Five Hundred ($2,500.00) Dollars. Professional Errors and Omissions Liability Insurance with policy limits of not less than One Million Dollars ($1,000,000.00) per claim with a 12-month maintenance period, a deductible of not more than $25,000 and with an aggregate limit of not less than $2,000,000. Cyber Liability Insurance providing cyber liability insurance, including coverage for cyber-related errors and omissions, media liability, network and information security liability, regulatory fines, penalties with associated forensic investigation expenses, and security breach remediation and notification, for a minimum of $2,000,000 per occurrence for claims for first and third-party costs or damages resulting from unauthorized access or disclosure, theft, dissemination, and/or use of confidential information or personal information stored or transmitted in any form resulting from any cyber events (including but not limited to network security breaches, privacy events, ransomware attacks) or arising during the performance of the Services.

### data_security_privacy_requirements
Ensure data security. Ensure all sensitive data is encrypted both at rest and in transit to protect against unauthorized access. Single Sign On (SSO) and/or Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Secure login with single sign-on or multi-factor authentication. Vendor Cybersecurity Compliance: Compliance with industry security best practices (SOC2, ISO, etc.). PIPA Compliance: Vendor server preferred within Canada to comply with PIPA. Incident Response Plan: Vendor has an incident response plan. Business Continuity Plan: Vendor has a business continuity and disaster recovery (BCP/DR) plan. Data Retention Policy: Clear data retention policy, including post-contract data management. Data Storage: Option for cloud storage in Canada or on-premises storage. Scalability: Ability to scale with the growing needs of the organization, including high availability, load balancing, and elastic scaling. Deployment: Flexible deployment options (cloud-based, on-premises, hybrid). Vulnerability Management: Regular pen tests, patch cadence, vulnerability disclosure program. Sandbox Environment: Continue to provide a sandbox environment throughout the lifecycle of the contract. Third-Party suppliers or subcontractors: Disclosure of third-party suppliers or subcontractors involved in service delivery. Security Incident Breaches: Disclosure of any past security incidents or breaches and remediation actions taken.
