### submission_method
Electronically via email to PublicBids@rockyview.ca; proposals must be in PDF format, prominently marked with the RFP title and number, and include the Submission Form from Appendix B.

### contract_term
Initial term until December 31, 2025, with an option to renew for up to one (1) additional year, for a total period not to exceed three (3) years.

---
### scope_of_deliverables
ERP Requirements Analysis and Readiness Review, including organizational readiness, technological capabilities, data management, workflow analysis, and gap analysis (Phase 1); development of a comprehensive RFP scope of work document (Phase 2); and support for the ERP vendor evaluation and selection process (Phase 3).

---
### mandatory_submission_requirements
Submission Form (Appendix B), Pricing Schedule (Appendix C), Mandatory Evaluation Table (Section B.3), and all issued addenda.

---
### mandatory_technical_requirements
Compliance with the Mandatory Evaluation Table (Section B.3); Project Manager with minimum 5 years ERP experience and 2 ERP projects in the last 7 years; Project team members with minimum 3 years ERP experience in the last 7 years; Minimum of three (3) public sector client references within the last 7 years; Budgetary control within 10% variance.

---
### evaluation_criteria
Proponent's Experience and Qualifications (10%), Service Team Qualifications and Experience (25%), Client References and Past Experience (10%), Work Plan, Methodology, and Quality Assurance (30%), and Pricing (25%). Total Points: 100.

### pricing_requirements
Rates in Canadian ($) funds, all-inclusive of labour, equipment, materials, travel, insurance, and taxes (except sales tax which must be itemized). Pricing must be provided in a Task-Fee Schedule (Unit price and Lump-sum) per project phase.

---
### minimum_insurance_requirements
All required insurance and documents must be included as per the Mandatory Evaluation Table (Section B.3).

---
### vendor_experience_qualifications
Project Manager: minimum 5 years ERP experience and 2 ERP projects in the last 7 years. Project team members: minimum 3 years ERP experience in the last 7 years. Experience with municipal-level public sector clients is preferred.

---
### number_of_references_required
Minimum of three (3) (preferably PUBLIC SECTOR) client references for similar/identical services within the last seven (7) years.

---
### data_security_privacy_requirements
Assessment of data management practices, data governance, quality, and security protocols; compliance with data protection regulations; and identification of security measures for cloud-based ERP operations.

---
### data_hosting_residency_requirements
Evaluation of data residency requirements and compliance with relevant regulations for cloud-stored data; assessment of the County's readiness for a potential cloud-based ERP solution.

---
### vendor_demonstration_requirement
Shortlisted vendors will be invited to provide a product demonstration and assist the County in clarifying queries prior to the demonstration process.
